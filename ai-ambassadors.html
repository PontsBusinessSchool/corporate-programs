Supercharge Your AI Ambassadors

Two days that turn your organization's AI ambassadors into people who build, support, and align.

Recommended minimum duration: 2 days

For: AI ambassadors nominated by their organization, from any function

Group rate:21 000 €excl. tax

Program Overview

Two days that turn your organization's AI ambassadors into people who build, support, and align.

Who it is for

AI ambassadors nominated by their organization, from any function: people with the credibility and the time to help colleagues change how they work. No technical background is needed, and tables mix levels so that those who already use AI help the others.

Day one: capability

The day opens with a live demonstration, applying AI to a real problem the group faces, with each table running a workflow of its own during it, and a hands-on prompting exercise covering technique, voice, and clear instruction. Ambassadors then build two agentic workflows, first a simple one and then a more complex one, each on a high-priority task from their own work, in the organization's own AI tool, so that what they build stays. Capability comes first, because an ambassador who has felt what AI can do supports colleagues far better than one who has only heard about it.

Day two: supporting others and aligning on next steps

Supporting others, through live practice. Ambassadors learn how to support others with AI tools and practice it in the room: each ambassador runs a short session and gets written feedback from three peers before running it at work.

Priorities and next steps. The group then aligns on priorities and next steps for AI adoption in the organization, with a 30-day plan for each participant.

What participants leave with

  1. Working agentic workflows, built by each ambassador on their own high-priority tasks.
  2. The ability to support their colleagues with AI tools, tested in live practice.
  3. Alignment on priorities and next steps for AI adoption in the organization, and a 30-day plan for each participant.

Extension

Coaching sessions for the ambassadors. Small-group coaching over the weeks after the program, side by side, to embed AI into their own tools and routines. Each session starts from a task they bring and ends with it running.

Train-the-trainer support. We rehearse each session with the ambassadors before they run it and review it with them afterward.

Facilitation of training sessions across the organization, alongside the ambassadors.

Format

Two consecutive days, in person at your premises or on the Paris campus, in French or English, in sequences of about 45 minutes.

Customization

Content, duration and language can be adapted to your organization. Your case studies, sector examples and internal scenarios can be incorporated during preparation.

How we teach

Our AI programs follow one principle: demonstration and doing, not theory. In half a day or two days, you work on a problem of your own, with your hands on the keyboard, and you leave with something running and a plan for what you do next. Eight principles apply to every program.

  1. Learning that sticks. Lasting retention, immediate application, and an experience built for executives that takes them out of their comfort zone.
  2. Short, dynamic sequences. About 45 minutes each, alternating method, practice, reflection, and application, with a five-minute pause before the next one.
  3. Experiential and concrete. Real cases chosen by the participants and linked to their business priorities. Every participant leaves with a concrete action plan.
  4. Peer learning and collective intelligence. Pairs and small groups that mix levels of AI maturity, with co-development and feedback from peers.
  5. Practitioners in the room. Recognized experts with complementary perspectives, including market practitioners who know organizations from the inside.
  6. Demonstration and doing, not theory. You see the work change rather than hearing about it, and your hands are on the keyboard in the first morning.
  7. Your problem, public information only, nothing hidden. Every exercise runs on a problem of yours, never on your data. Every tool is driven by hand, on screen, and something will fail in front of the room: that is part of the lesson.
  8. How we teach you to use AI. We do not teach a tool. We teach you to build the system that lets you put ideas together far faster than by hand, the way a sewing machine changed sewing. AI is not a colleague: it is a machine you brief, direct, and check.
